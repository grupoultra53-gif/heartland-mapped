3. YOUTUBE SHORTS — DESCRICAO (COPIAR E COLAR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How did one stubborn farmer bend Missouri's southern border straight into Arkansas? In 1818, Congress drafted Missouri's state map as a clean rectangle ending along the 36-30 parallel—dumping the southeastern river lowlands into raw Arkansas Territory.

Wealthy cattle rancher John Hardeman Walker—known as the "Czar of the Valley"—refused to live under frontier territorial courts instead of statehood. After a fierce lobbying blitz, Congress dropped the border 34 miles south between the St. Francis and Mississippi rivers in 1821, creating Missouri's 980-square-mile Bootheel. Walker even became the county's first sheriff and judge inside the border he invented!
