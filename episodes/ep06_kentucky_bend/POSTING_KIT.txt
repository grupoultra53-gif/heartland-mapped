3. YOUTUBE SHORTS — DESCRICAO (COPIAR E COLAR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why is there a chunk of Kentucky completely trapped inside Tennessee and Missouri? In 1779, surveyors stopped the Kentucky–Tennessee border short of the Mississippi River. After the massive 1811–1812 New Madrid earthquakes twisted the river's path, the finished line sliced straight across a giant double horseshoe bend—leaving 30 square miles of Kentucky completely detached.

Surrounded on three sides by the river and Missouri, the only road out leads south into Tennessee—meaning residents drive 40 miles through another state just to reach their own Fulton County courthouse. Even Mark Twain wrote about it!
